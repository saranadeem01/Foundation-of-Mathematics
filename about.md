# Foundation of Mathematics

**Undergraduate Course | Information Technology University (ITU), Pakistan**

Foundation of Mathematics is an undergraduate course designed for students with a Pre-Medical background transitioning into quantitative fields such as Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, and related disciplines.

The course provides a structured introduction to the mathematical concepts and problem-solving skills required for university-level study. It emphasizes conceptual understanding, mathematical intuition, graphical interpretation, and problem solving, rather than relying solely on formula memorization.

Foundation of Mathematics serves as a prerequisite for Calculus-I.
---

## 📚 Course Overview

The course begins with fundamental mathematical concepts and gradually progresses toward introductory calculus and related topics.

The main areas covered include:

- Number systems and basic mathematical concepts
- Algebra and equations
- Coordinate geometry
- Lines, slopes, and graphs
- Functions and their representations
- Domain and range
- Polynomial, rational, exponential, and logarithmic functions
- Function transformations
- Trigonometric functions
- Limits and continuity
- Differentiation and applications of derivatives
- Integration and applications of integrals
- Sequences and infinite series

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of the course, students should be able to:

- Understand and interpret fundamental mathematical concepts.
- Solve algebraic equations and inequalities.
- Work with functions and analyze their graphs.
- Understand domain, range, and transformations of functions.
- Interpret mathematical relationships graphically and algebraically.
- Understand the fundamental concepts of limits and continuity.
- Compute and interpret derivatives as rates of change.
- Apply differentiation to optimization and related problems.
- Understand integration as accumulation and area.
- Apply basic techniques of integration.
- Understand sequences, series, and their convergence.
- Develop the mathematical foundation required for subsequent undergraduate courses.

---

## 🧭 Course Structure

The course follows a gradual progression:

**Mathematical Foundations → Algebra → Coordinate Geometry → Functions → Trigonometry → Limits → Derivatives → Integrals → Sequences & Series**

The emphasis throughout the course is on connecting mathematical concepts with their **graphical, algebraic, and intuitive interpretations**.

---

## 💡 Learning Approach

A central goal of the course is to help students develop **mathematical intuition**.

Concepts are explored from multiple perspectives:

**Graphical → Algebraic → Geometric → Intuitive → Formal**

This approach is particularly useful for students transitioning from a Pre-Medical background to undergraduate programs that require substantial mathematical reasoning.
